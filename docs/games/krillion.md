# Krillion

A game where you try to pick the least-commonly-guessed answer in some category over 7 timed, 25-second rounds.

Each time, you get a score based on how "deep" your answer is and you "dive" to some depth. The whole thing is
nautical-themed, so you get a Krill or a Sturgeon or whatever as your score for each round, in addition to some "depth"
in meters.

## How to Play

<ul><li>7 prompts a day. same for everyone.</li><li>25 seconds to name one thing.</li><li>rare answers score big. obvious ones float.</li><li>the pick that feels clever? the school thought of it too.</li><li>every point sinks you 10 metres. 700 is the trench.</li><li>one dive per day. no second chances.</li></ul>
